## At the review desk

Mara is the fictional instructor for this invented SlopU course. The teaching role is to ask three questions: where does this state live, who can change it, and what evidence would let another developer change it safely?

Bring the smallest reproduction you can make, its expected result and the result you actually observed. Screenshots help with layout; a state trace helps with ownership. The weekly studio reserves time for that review.
