## At the review desk

I’m Guochen Wang, an ANU Bachelor of Advanced Computing (Honours) student with a Machine Learning Specialisation. I’m interested in frontend development and build Vue, React and TypeScript applications with a focus on clean interfaces and polished user experiences.

This course follows the same questions I use when learning and building: where does this state live, who can change it, and what evidence lets another developer change it safely? Bring a small reproduction, its expected result and the result you actually observed. Screenshots help with layout; a state trace helps with ownership.

I contribute to the [Techlauncher-LeafAnalyzer organisation](https://github.com/Techlauncher-LeafAnalyzer) and keep notes, demos and experiments visible through hands-on projects. Explore my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/u7663394) for current work.
